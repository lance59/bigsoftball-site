PROGRAM AT A GLANCE

What is Select?

A competitive supplement for girls in U13 House League. Features indoor winter training, upgraded team uniforms, and regional weekend tournaments.

Time Commitment

Winter: 1–2 indoor sessions/week focused on pitching, hitting, and fielding mechanics.

Summer: Base House League schedule (2 weeknights/week) + Select weekend tournaments and targeted team practices.

Season Timeline

Winter Training: Dec – Apr (Indoor Practices)
Summer Season: May – Aug (Practies, Games & Tournaments)

Core Focus

Advanced fundamental mechanics, tactical softball IQ, positional mastery, and building a positive, resilient team culture.

THE STANDARD

01

DEVELOPMENT

Relentless focus on fundamental mechanics, position-specific skill, and multi-position field experience—developing well-rounded athletes, not single-position specialists.

02

COMPETITION

The best of both worlds: local House League play paired with regional Select weekend tournaments to elevate game IQ and test skills against top competition.

03

CULTURE

Accountability, grit, and a team-first attitude on and off the field. We play bold and respect the game.
